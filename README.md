## English

### Overview

Generative Models Atlas is a curated collection of paper notes dedicated to understanding influential generative modeling methods. Rather than attempting to cover every published paper, this repository focuses on representative works that have shaped the field.

Each note goes beyond a conventional paper summary to examine the core idea, mathematical formulation, methodological contributions, limitations, and potential applications of the proposed approach.

The goal is to build a concise, evolving knowledge base that helps readers understand generative models, compare different methodological choices, and identify useful ideas for their own research.

### Topics

The repository covers major families of generative models, including:

- **Diffusion Models and Score-based Generative Models**
- **Flow Matching and Rectified Flows**
- **Variational Autoencoders (VAEs)**
- **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)**
- **Normalizing Flows**
- **Autoregressive Generative Models**
- **Other Influential Generative Modeling Methods**

The scope will evolve as new foundational methods and research directions emerge.

### Analysis Framework

Depending on the paper, each note may cover:

1. **Background and Motivation** — What problem does the paper address, and why does it matter?
2. **Core Idea** — What is the central insight behind the proposed method?
3. **Mathematical Foundations** — What are the key equations, assumptions, and theoretical connections?
4. **Methodological Contributions** — What is genuinely new compared with previous approaches?
5. **Strengths and Limitations** — What are the method's advantages, assumptions, and limitations?
6. **Practical Applications** — How can the method be applied or adapted to different research problems?
7. **Connections to Other Methods** — How does it relate to other generative modeling approaches?

The emphasis is on understanding the reasoning behind each method rather than merely reproducing its abstract or experimental results.

### Scope and Philosophy

- **Curated, not exhaustive.** Prioritize influential and conceptually important papers over sheer quantity.
- **Understand, not just summarize.** Explain the underlying mechanisms and mathematical intuition.
- **Analyze, not just reproduce.** Examine assumptions, limitations, and practical implications.
- **Connect theory with applications.** Explore how generative modeling techniques can inform and support research in different domains.

## 中文

### 项目简介

**生成模型图谱（Generative Models Atlas）** 是一个专注于生成模型代表性论文解读与应用分析的精选知识库。

本仓库不追求覆盖所有已发表的论文，而是重点整理那些具有代表性、对领域发展产生重要影响，或能够帮助理解生成建模核心思想的研究工作。

每篇笔记不仅介绍论文的主要内容，还会进一步分析其核心思想、数学原理、方法创新、适用条件、局限性及潜在应用。

本项目希望建立一个简洁、持续演进的知识库，帮助读者理解生成模型的基本原理、比较不同方法的设计思路，并从已有研究中寻找能够应用于自身研究的问题与技术。

### 研究方向

本仓库主要关注以下生成模型及相关方法：

- **扩散模型与基于 Score 的生成模型**
- **流匹配与整流流**
- **变分自编码器（VAE）**
- **生成对抗网络（GAN）**
- **归一化流**
- **自回归生成模型**
- **其他具有代表性的生成建模方法**

随着生成建模领域的发展，仓库内容将围绕新的代表性方法和重要研究方向逐步扩展。

### 解读框架

根据论文的具体内容，每篇笔记将从以下角度展开分析：

1. **研究背景与动机**：论文试图解决什么问题，为什么这一问题值得研究？
2. **核心思想**：所提出方法最关键的思路是什么？
3. **数学原理**：核心公式、建模假设及其理论联系是什么？
4. **方法创新**：与已有方法相比，真正的创新点在哪里？
5. **优势与局限**：方法具有什么优势、依赖哪些假设，又存在哪些不足？
6. **实际应用**：该方法可以如何应用或迁移到不同的研究问题中？
7. **与其他方法的联系**：该方法与其他生成模型之间存在怎样的联系与区别？

本仓库强调对方法原理和设计逻辑的理解，而不仅仅是对论文摘要或实验结果的复述。

### 项目原则

- **精选而非穷举。** 优先关注具有重要影响或理论价值的代表性论文，而非单纯追求数量。
- **重在理解，而非摘要。** 解释方法背后的机制、数学原理与设计直觉。
- **注重分析，而非复述。** 讨论建模假设、适用条件、方法局限与实际意义。
- **连接理论与应用。** 探索生成建模方法在不同研究领域中的应用潜力。
